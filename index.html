About Us

01 / GENERAL

Right & Tight Plumbing was built on one idea: do the job correctly the first time, so nobody has to think about their pipes again.

Our owner, Jerry Hunt, has spent more than 20 years working on residential and commercial systems in and around Dallas-Fort Worth — from single-fixture repairs to full home and commercial repipes. Every job still gets a hands-on visit from a licensed master plumber, not a rotating crew.

We show up on time, explain what we find in plain language before any work starts, and stand behind every fitting we tighten. That's the whole business plan.
